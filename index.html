Research Focus

My work spans human-centered computer vision and responsible machine learning, particularly where real-world data are imperfect, demographically diverse, or visually degraded.

Research Area 01

Affective Computing

Facial expression recognition, emotion-aware representations, culturally diverse datasets, and robust recognition in realistic conditions.

Research Area 02

Image Enhancement

Single-image and face super-resolution, refinement of low-quality visual inputs, and emotion-preserving visual restoration.

Research Area 03

Fairness in AI

Bias analysis and mitigation, latent representation alignment, demographic fairness, and equitable evaluation of AI systems.
