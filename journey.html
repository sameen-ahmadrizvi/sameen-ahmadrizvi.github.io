Biography

Research, teaching and practical AI

Dr. Syed Sameen Ahmad Rizvi is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at SRM University–AP. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from BITS Pilani, where his research focused on computer vision and deep learning.

His research spans affective computing, image enhancement, trustworthy artificial intelligence, multimodal learning, natural language processing and theoretical machine learning, with an emphasis on translating methodological advances into practical real-world systems. His work has appeared in venues including AAAI, ICPR, IEEE Open Journal of the Computer Society and Cognitive Computation.

Alongside research, he is actively involved in undergraduate teaching and mentoring students in research-oriented projects, guiding them through problem formulation, literature review, experimental design, implementation, reproducibility and technical communication.
