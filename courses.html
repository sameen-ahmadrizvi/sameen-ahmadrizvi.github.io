Courses Taught

I aim to make technically demanding material approachable without diluting rigor, connecting theoretical foundations with implementation, problem solving and independent thinking.

Teaching Philosophy

Rigor, reasoning and implementation

I view teaching as a balance between conceptual clarity and technical depth. Classroom discussions establish the foundations; laboratory and project work then ask students to implement, test and reason about those ideas. My assessments emphasize understanding over memorization, while student projects encourage reproducibility, technical communication and independent problem solving.
