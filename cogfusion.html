Research Areas

CogFusion brings together complementary research directions while keeping a strong focus on rigorous machine learning and practical impact.

01

Vision

Computer vision, affective computing, facial analysis, image enhancement, super-resolution, multimodal visual understanding, and responsible visual AI.

02

Natural Language Processing

Language understanding and generation, educational AI, question generation, retrieval-augmented systems, and multimodal language-centered applications.

03

ML Theory

Learning algorithms, representation learning, robustness, fairness, efficient learning, and investigations into alternative training paradigms.

04

Applied AI

Translating AI methods into useful systems and interdisciplinary applications through reproducible experimentation and student-led research.
